# Homepage promotion photography

These four photo backgrounds were generated with the built-in image generation tool using the supplied two-column Promotions screenshot as the composition reference. Original PNGs are preserved in `output/imagegen/promotions-reference-v2/`. WebP copies are used by the homepage only.

- `sports.webp`: an adult basketball player in a dark green jersey, in an indoor arena; dark emerald negative space on the left.
- `slots.webp`: a gold trophy and casino chips on burgundy velvet; dark red negative space on the left.
- `live-casino.webp`: an adult live dealer reaching over a roulette wheel; purple-black negative space on the left.
- `rankings.webp`: an emerald gift box, gold ribbon, and a physical leaderboard on dark marble; dark green negative space on the left.

Each image is 2172 by 724 pixels. Promotional titles, rates, and links remain HTML text. Border palettes are assigned by the semantic photo tone in `allgames-home.css`.
